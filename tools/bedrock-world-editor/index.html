How It Works

Three steps, no install.

1 · Open

Drop a .mcworld or pick the world folder. The zip is unpacked in memory and level.dat is decoded as Bedrock NBT.

2 · Edit

Use the friendly World and Player tabs, or dive into the raw NBT tree and file browser for anything else.

3 · Export

The archive is rebuilt and downloaded as .mcworld. Open it on your device and Minecraft imports it.
