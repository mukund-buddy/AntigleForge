How it works

        
The 75% math, done for you

      


      

        

          

            
Current percentage

            
Attended ÷ held × 100 gives the running number your college uses. The calculator shows it immediately — no mental division.

          

        

        

          

            
Can you skip the next class?

            
With a planned total entered, the calculator works backwards from the threshold to show how many of the remaining classes you can miss — and how many you must still attend.

          

        

        

          

            
Threshold is your call

            
75% is the default because most colleges require it, but the slider covers 50–90% if your rule differs.

          

        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        
Questions

        
Attendance FAQ

      

      

        

          What if I already missed the threshold?
          
The calculator still shows your current percentage and how many classes you would need to attend in a row to climb back — but only if the remaining classes are enough to reach it.

        

        

          Can I use this for something other than classes?
          
Yes — any attended/total situation works: gym sessions, project meetings, or team events. The threshold slider adapts the planning math to whatever rule you set.

        

        

          Why does the planning need the total classes planned?
          
Skip/attend planning predicts the end-of-term percentage, and that needs a denominator for the whole term. Without it, only the current percentage is computed.
