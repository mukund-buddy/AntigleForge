How it works

        
Rules, not language models

      


      

        

          

            
Sentences become blanks

            
The text is split into sentences. In each one the most distinctive content word — usually the longest, or a proper name — is replaced with ____. Short fragments are skipped so the sheet stays meaningful.

          

        

        

          

            
Options come from your own words

            
For the correct answer the tool pulls other words of similar length or function from the rest of your material, so the wrong answers look and read like the right one. Loaded options are then shuffled deterministically.

          

        

        

          

            
It ends with an answer key

            
Every one of those questions is matched in the “Answer key” section with its correct option and the exact answer word. Keep the key in your notes, quiz yourself later, and the recall will stick.

          

        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        
Questions

        
MCQ generator FAQ

      

      

        

          Is this AI-generated?
          
No. The questions are built by clear rules running in your browser: pick a meaningful word in each sentence, blank it, and draw look-alike wrong options from the rest of your text. It never calls a server and never uses an AI.

        

        

          Which input format works best?
          
Paste notes as full sentences for fill-in-the-blank questions, or write lines in the form Question? Answer for a classic quiz. The tool detects the format itself — no choosing involved.

        

        

          Why do questions sometimes have different numbers of options?
          
Options come from similar words inside your text. If the text is short, there may not be enough safe alternatives for a given keyword, so the generator stops at the number it can guarantee. Every question still has at least two options and its answer in the key.
