List Sorter

Paste any list — names, URLs, prices, IDs — and sort it alphabetically or numerically, straight or reversed, with optional deduplication or a random shuffle. All instant, all local.

How it works

        
Order, without the tedium

      


      

        

          

            
Alpha vs numeric

            
Alphabetical sorting handles words and mixed content like a dictionary. Numeric sorting parses each line as a number so that 10 correctly follows 2, and keeps text rows at the end of the result.

          

        

        

          

            
Combined operations

            
Dedupe, reverse and shuffle combine freely. Reversing flips the final order; shuffle replaces sorting entirely and gives you a random arrangement every time.

          

        

        

          

            
Local only

            
Nothing is sent anywhere — paste names, emails, prices or tracking IDs and sort them knowing they stay on your machine.

          

        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        
Questions

        
List sorter FAQ

      

      

        

          How does numeric sorting place 10 and 2 correctly?
          
Each line is parsed as a number; 2 comes before 10 numerically instead of alphabetically. Lines that are not numbers stay in the output sorted after the numbers.

        

        

          What are the dedupe modes?
          
Exact removes lines that are identical. Trim treats leading/trailing whitespace as invisible. Case-insensitive ignores letter casing (Apple and apple are the same).

        

        

          Is my list uploaded anywhere?
          
No — everything runs locally in your browser. Your data never leaves the tab.
