    How it works
    
        
    How the title length checker works
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Count against the real limit
    
            
    YouTube caps video titles at 100 characters. The counter shows where you stand with a live progress bar.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Spot emoji before they bite
    
            
    Emoji are wider than letters and can wrap or truncate titles that technically "fit". The checker flags them so you can decide deliberately.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Preview truncation
    
            
    Many surfaces show only around 70 characters. A preview shows what a truncated title would look like — put the important words first.
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Questions
    
        
    Title length checker FAQ
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          What is the YouTube title character limit?
          
    Video titles can contain up to 100 characters. The checker counts your title live and shows how close you are, plus a preview of how it may be cut on surfaces that display fewer characters.
    
        
    
        
    
          Why do emojis make my title look shorter than it is?
          
    Emoji and other wide glyphs occupy more pixel width than letters, and some render as multi-character sequences. The checker flags emoji so you know a title that 'fits' by character count may still wrap or truncate visually.
    
        
    
        
    
          How long should a YouTube title be?
          
    YouTube accepts up to 100 characters, but most titles are shown fully only at around 70 characters on common surfaces. Aim for 60–70 characters with the important words first.
    
        
    
        
    
          Is the title length checker free?
          
    Yes. Every tool on AntigleForge is free and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or processed on a server.
