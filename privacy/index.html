Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

The short version: our tools run entirely in your browser. We have no backend, no accounts, and nothing is uploaded to our servers — because there are no servers.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data through our tools. The tools process text and files exclusively on your device — nothing you type, paste, upload, or generate is transmitted to us. Because the platform is a static website with no backend or database, there is nothing to store.

Last updated: 2026-08-06.

Hosting, logs, and analytics

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. As with any hosted website, the hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (IP addresses, requested pages, timestamps) for security and operational purposes in line with its own privacy policy.

We do not currently run our own analytics. If site analytics are enabled in the future, only privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics will be used, and this policy will be updated.

Cookies and advertising

The website itself sets no cookies. Advertising depends on the ad provider shown on the site, which may use cookies to measure ad performance; the provider's own privacy and cookie policies apply on their ads. We never use pop-up, pop-under, or interstitial ads, and we never show adult content.

If you block cookies or use a private browsing mode, all tools continue to work normally.

External links

Outbound links (for example, premium resources sold on external stores, or our social profiles) are clearly labeled and open with secure, no-referrer attributes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Contact us.

Disclaimer

AntigleForge is an independent creator platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios or Microsoft. While we aim for accuracy, tools are provided "as is" without warranty of any kind — always test generated output before using it in production projects.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform grows. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
