LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Short version: there is no account, no analytics and no upload. Your input stays in your browser.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. AntigleForge has no accounts, no sign-up, no contact forms, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive the coordinates, commands, JSON or text you type into any tool.

How your input is handled

All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Generated commands, manifests, loot tables and files are created on your device and are only stored if you download them yourself.

Data stored on your device

Two small preferences are kept in your browser's localStorage:

  • antigleforge_perf_mode — remembers whether you turned Performance Mode on.
  • antigleforge_lang — remembers the language you picked in the header.

These stay on your device, are never transmitted, and are removed when you clear your browser storage.

Third-party requests

To keep the site fast we load fonts from Google Fonts. That request shares your IP address with Google as part of normal web delivery, but sends none of your tool input. Links to YouTube and GitHub only contact those sites when you click them.

Cookies

AntigleForge sets no cookies.

Children

The site is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Questions can be sent through the YouTube channel linked in the footer.
