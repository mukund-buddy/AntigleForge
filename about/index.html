Who we are

About AntigleForge

A growing platform of fast, free browser tools — starting with the Minecraft Bedrock community that built our brand, and expanding into web development, student tools, business tools, and creator resources.

Built for creators, built to be free

The Antigle started in the Minecraft community, producing videos and resources for Bedrock players. This tools platform is the next chapter: every utility we wish existed — free, in the browser, with no accounts and no data leaving your device.

Premium prompt packs, templates, and design assets are available through trusted external stores. We keep the platform itself free; premium is always a clear, honest link — never a fake button.
