About AntigleForge

Free Minecraft tools that run entirely in your browser.

What is AntigleForge?

AntigleForge is a collection of free tools for Minecraft Bedrock and Java players, builders and add-on creators. You get coordinate calculators, command builders, generators for manifests, loot tables and recipes, plus formatting and conversion utilities.

How it works

Every tool is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Calculations, conversions and file generation happen inside your browser tab. There is no backend, no account, and no upload step — your coordinates, JSON and text never leave your device.

What you will find

  • Player tools — Nether portal coordinates, command builders, XP, enchanting, villager trades and farm calculators.
  • Creator tools — Manifest, loot table, recipe and spawn rule generators, plus item lore and tellraw builders.
  • Utilities — UUID generator, colour converter, shape generator and building material calculator.

Who makes it

AntigleForge is built and maintained by Mukund. Tool requests, bug reports and feature ideas are always welcome on the YouTube channel or the GitHub profile linked in the footer.
